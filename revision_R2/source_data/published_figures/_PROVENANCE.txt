2. Data derived from these figures (adaptations)
------------------------------------------------
outputs/E15_digitised_curves.csv, E15_axis_calibration.csv and E24_digitised_Zhang2026.csv contain values
read from the images above by the scripts e15_digitise_published_cleaning_data.py and
e24_tests_beyond_calibration_data.py (colour segmentation and axis calibration on the tick/grid positions;
no manual point picking). They are approximations of the plotted data points, made for this study (2026);
they are not the original measurements of the authors, who have not reviewed or endorsed the digitised
values or the analyses based on them. These derived files are made available under CC BY 4.0; when
reusing them, cite the original article and state that the values were digitised from its figures.
outputs/E15_published_scalar_observations.csv lists single values quoted from the text of the two
articles (Zhang et al., 2025; Wiese et al., 2024), with their location.

3. Values quoted from articles that are not open access
--------------------------------------------------------
The script e24_tests_beyond_calibration_data.py and its outputs (E24_plateau_flux_other_laboratories.csv,
E24_published_sensitivities.csv) quote individual numerical results, with their source and location, from:
  Gillham, C.R., Fryer, P.J., Hasting, A.P.M., Wilson, D.I. (1999). Food and Bioproducts Processing 77,
    127-136. https://doi.org/10.1205/096030899532420 (Table 3, isothermal experiments; activation energies
    and Reynolds exponent stated by the authors)
  Xin, H., Chen, X.D., Ozkan, N. (2004). AIChE Journal 50, 1961-1973. https://doi.org/10.1002/aic.10149
    (velocity, temperature and uniform-stage rate of Tables 1 and 2; activation energies of Table 3)
  Tuladhar, T.R., Paterson, W.R., Wilson, D.I. (2002). Food and Bioproducts Processing 80, 199-214.
    https://doi.org/10.1205/096030802760309223 (cleaning times, activation energies and reaction orders
    stated in the text)
Only numerical results (individual values and a few rows of tables) are quoted, for comparison and with citation. No figure, table layout or
text of these articles is reproduced, and the articles themselves are not distributed here; they remain
under the copyright of their publishers.

4. Not used
-----------
No material was taken from sources whose terms require permission for reuse (e.g. theses with a
copyright statement), and no plant-derived, confidential or proprietary data are included.
